My understanding of Zayo Opportunity

The Core Challenge

Zayo's premier assets and market leadership are constrained by a fractured technical foundation inherited from years of growth through acquisition.

Zayo's Strength: A Diverse Portfolio

  • 📡
    Dark Fiber: The foundational asset—leasing raw, unlit fiber infrastructure.
  • 💡
    Wavelengths: Providing managed, lit-fiber capacity at specific speeds.
  • 🌐
    Private Data Networks (WAN): Creating secure, private networks for enterprises.
  • ↔️
    Ethernet & DIA: Offering flexible, high-performance connectivity.

The Central Problem: Systemic Complexity

A Tangled Web from M&A: Successive acquisitions have created a web of redundant systems for:

  • Network Inventory: No single source of truth for fiber assets.
  • Service Provisioning: Manual, "swivel-chair" workflows.
  • Quoting & Billing: Inconsistent pricing and error-prone invoicing.

The Problem Statement for the Business Architect:

How do we rationalize the complex web of inventory, provisioning, and billing systems acquired over years to create a single, efficient engine that can deliver *any* of these products seamlessly, starting with the core Dark Fiber capability?
